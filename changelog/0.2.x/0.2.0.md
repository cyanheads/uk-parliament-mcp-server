# 0.2.0 — 2026-11-15

## Added

- **`ukparl_search_statements`** searches written ministerial statements by text, UIN, minister, House, answering body and date made; `mode=detail` returns one by `statementId` ([#12](https://github.com/cyanheads/uk-parliament-mcp-server/issues/12)).
- **`skill://uk-parliament-guide/SKILL.md`**, the first root `skills/` tree, is served in `resources/list` with the identifier chains and reporting gates for the `ukparl_*` tools.
- **`ukparl_get_committee` `mode=evidence_document`** returns an evidence record plus its published Html rendition as `documentHtml`; evidence and publication `DocumentFile` entries gain `apiRoute` ([#19](https://github.com/cyanheads/uk-parliament-mcp-server/issues/19)).
- **`ukparl_search_hansard` `sortOrder`** takes `SittingDateAsc` or `SittingDateDesc`; omitted keeps newest sitting first, and a cursor binds to the order it was minted under ([#20](https://github.com/cyanheads/uk-parliament-mcp-server/issues/20)).

## Changed

- **Collection pages return the caller's `limit`** in whole records instead of being cut to a 24,000-byte response budget; only a single record over 24,000 bytes uses `delivery: outline|fragment`. Caps are now 100 for `ukparl_search_bills`, `ukparl_search_hansard`, `ukparl_search_questions`, `ukparl_get_committee` and `ukparl_get_bill` collections, and 24 for Commons division search (Lords 10); `cap` in notices reports that ceiling, not the effective limit ([#18](https://github.com/cyanheads/uk-parliament-mcp-server/issues/18)).
- **Search payloads carry `source` once**, `ukparl_search_bills` included, instead of on every item ([#18](https://github.com/cyanheads/uk-parliament-mcp-server/issues/18)).
- **Output schemas are shared** through `$defs`/`$ref`, so `tools/list` is 267,492 B for ten tools (0.1.2: 354,951 B for nine) and the `ukparl_get_committee` entry 62,655 B (116,860 B); no field or validation outcome changes ([#13](https://github.com/cyanheads/uk-parliament-mcp-server/issues/13)).
- **Slow requests run on one attempt up to the 45 s call deadline** and fail as `call_deadline_exceeded`; `attempt_timeout` is retired. Every upstream 5xx carries `reason: http_server_error`, retried only while the remaining budget covers another attempt, and all ten tools declare both reasons ([#17](https://github.com/cyanheads/uk-parliament-mcp-server/issues/17)).

## Removed

- Per-item `source` on `ukparl_search_bills`, `ukparl_search_members`, `ukparl_search_divisions`, `ukparl_search_hansard` and `ukparl_search_questions` items, and `record.source` in division and question detail; use the payload `source` ([#18](https://github.com/cyanheads/uk-parliament-mcp-server/issues/18)).
- `rosterAvailableVia`, `headlineCountTreatment` and `headlineCountExplanationUrl` on division search items; division `mode=detail` still carries them ([#18](https://github.com/cyanheads/uk-parliament-mcp-server/issues/18)).
- Committee and business entities on written-evidence items in `ukparl_get_committee` `mode=evidence`, and the committee entity and `committeePath` on publication items in `mode=publications`; `evidence_detail`, `publication` and `detail` still carry them ([#18](https://github.com/cyanheads/uk-parliament-mcp-server/issues/18)).

## Fixed

- **`inAmendableStage: false`** on `ukparl_search_bills` and **`acceptingEvidence: false`** on `ukparl_get_committee` inquiries now equal omission: they are no longer echoed as applied filters or bound into cursors ([#21](https://github.com/cyanheads/uk-parliament-mcp-server/issues/21)).
- **Commons deferred divisions** (`IsDeferred: true`, no tellers) return complete voters and party totals instead of a missing-roster notice.
- **`ukparl_search_members`** descriptions and its `asOf` notice say names, `latestParty` and `latestHouseMembership` are as they stood on `asOf`.
- **`invalid_cursor` and `source_changed`** errors carry each tool's own declared recovery hint instead of a generic one; the `source_changed` hint keeps the collection cursor.

## Upgrade notes

- Collection cursors from 0.1.x resume at their offset under any `limit`, with these exceptions: `ukparl_lookup_reference`, `ukparl_search_divisions` (search and voters), and cursors minted with `inAmendableStage: false` (`ukparl_search_bills`) or `acceptingEvidence: false` (`ukparl_get_committee` inquiries). Those fail once with `invalid_cursor`; repeat the call without `cursor`.
- A `contentCursor` from 0.1.x fails once with `source_changed` or `invalid_cursor` on every tool except `ukparl_get_bill`, `ukparl_get_member`, and `ukparl_get_committee` search, members (explicit limit) and detail. Repeat the call without `contentCursor`. Records under 24,000 bytes now arrive whole.
- An HTTP 504 from an official API is now ServiceUnavailable (`-32000`, `http_server_error`) instead of Timeout (`-32004`). Every 5xx carries `reason: http_server_error`. A slow call ends as `call_deadline_exceeded` at 45 s on one attempt, replacing the retired `attempt_timeout`.
- `ukparl_get_committee` collection modes default to 10 records; members, inquiries, publications and evidence were 20.
